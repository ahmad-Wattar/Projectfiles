## Master's Thesis

### Automated Extraction of Tumor Markers from German Pathology Reports

Master's thesis project at Martin Luther University Halle-Wittenberg, investigating methods for extracting tumor marker information from German pathology reports from the Cancer Registry of Saxony-Anhalt.

The proposal outlines a comparison of rule-based methods, prompt engineering, and fine-tuning of local large language models. It also explores the potential of embedding-based similarity metrics for evaluating extraction quality without a gold standard.

- [Thesis proposal / Exposé (July 2026, German)](Master_Thesis/Masterarbeit_expose.pdf)
- [Proposal presentation (August 2026, German)](Master_Thesis/Masterarbeit_expose_Vortrag.pdf)

These documents describe the proposed research scope; the final methods and results may differ.
